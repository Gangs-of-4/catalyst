# Onboarding questions — _template

Ask these when setting up a new client. Answers go into tenant config (not the repo) and `context/go-live-checklist.md` (roles only).

## Business
1. Business name as the agent should say it.
2. Address and landmark (for "kahan hai?").
3. Opening hours per weekday; weekly off; known holidays.

## Resources and services
4. Who/what can be booked (`labels.resource`)? Hours per resource.
5. Services offered; default slot length.

## Calls
6. Answer mode (pilot: missed + after-hours).
7. Backup chain: ordered separate mobile numbers and ring time each. (Not the forwarded business number.)
8. Who may send WhatsApp commands?

## Knowledge
9. Top 10 questions callers ask, with approved answers.
10. Things the agent must never say.

## Safety
11. Red-flag list and after-hours instruction (vertical-specific).

## Consent and baseline
12. Consent / recording wording.
13. One-week missed-call baseline before go-live.
